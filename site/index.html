› About XP Waste

XP Waste is a cozy idle skilling game for iPhone and iPad. Tap thematic training objects to level all 23 skills toward 99, set skills to train passively while you're away, and bank Energy for Supercharge bursts. It's a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

› Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my in-app purchases?

Token packs are consumable purchases (they're spent as you use them), so they can't be "restored" like a subscription. If you completed a purchase but didn't receive your tokens, please reach out through the support link above with your App Store receipt and we'll sort it out.

Where is my progress saved? Is it in the cloud?

Your progress is stored locally on your device. XP Waste does not require an account and does not sync to the cloud, so deleting the app will erase your progress.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. XP Waste is fully playable offline. An internet connection is only used when you choose to make an optional in-app purchase.

What are the in-app purchases?

XP Waste is free to play. Optional token packs (Pouch, Sack, and Chest of Tokens) can be spent in-app on Daily Boost coupons and Energy Cells to speed up your bursts. Everything the tokens buy can also be earned for free by playing.

How do I reset my progress?

You can reset your account from the in-app Settings tab. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What data do you collect?

None. XP Waste has no accounts, no analytics, no third-party trackers, and no ads. The app collects no personal data and makes no network requests other than to Apple's App Store for optional purchases.

› Privacy

XP Waste does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All game progress lives locally on your device. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.
